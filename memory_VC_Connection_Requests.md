# Task Memory: VC Connection Requests

## Objective
Provide short, punchy one-line connection request notes for Venture Capitalists (VCs) on LinkedIn.

## Strategy
VCs care about a few specific things: massive market size, the "picks and shovels" infrastructure layer, and protecting their existing portfolio. The message needs to convey that LexOculus is solving an urgent, highly technical problem (EU AI Act) that directly impacts B2B revenue.

## One-Line Options

**Option 1: The "Picks & Shovels" Angle (Best for Infrastructure VCs)**
"Hi [Name], building LexOculus—the automated 'picks and shovels' devtool for EU AI Act compliance. Would love to connect."

**Option 2: The "Portfolio Protection" Angle (Best for General AI VCs)**
"Building LexOculus to automate EU AI Act codebase audits—protecting AI startups from the new European regulatory blockade. Let’s connect."

**Option 3: The "Speed/ROI" Angle (Best for B2B SaaS VCs)**
"Hey [Name], building LexOculus to turn EU AI Act compliance from a 3-month manual legal audit into a 5-minute automated CI/CD scan. Would love to connect."

**Option 4: The Direct Market Maker**
"Hi [Name], I'm building LexOculus to automate technical compliance for the EU AI Act. Would love to share notes on the AI regulatory infrastructure space."

## The "Shock Value" Options (High Risk/High Reward)

**Option 5: The "Illegal Code" Hook**
"Hey [Name], 90% of the AI startups you fund are actively shipping illegal or 'High-Risk' code under the new EU AI Act. We built the automated technical audit to fix it. Let's connect."

**Option 6: The "Regulatory Liability" Hook**
"Hi [Name], the EU AI Act just turned half your AI portfolio into 'High-Risk' compliance liabilities. We provide the automated infrastructure to protect them before the fines hit. Let's connect."

**Option 7: The "Procurement Sabotage" Hook**
"Most AI tools you fund are about to fail European enterprise procurement because they unknowingly built 'High-Risk' architectures. We automate their compliance. Would love to connect."
