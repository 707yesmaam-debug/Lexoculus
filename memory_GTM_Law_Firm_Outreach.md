# Task Memory: GTM Law Firm Outreach

## Objective
Pivot the Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy to target EU-based boutique law firms (instead of founders directly) as channel partners to increase client acquisition and battle-test the platform.

## Key Actions Taken
1. **Strategic Reframing**: 
   - Shifted value proposition from "We keep your startup compliant" to "We help you offer highly profitable AI compliance audits at scale."
   - Highlighted LexOculus's ability to automate technical audits, generate white-labeled PDF reports, and provide court-ready technical evidence for legal teams.

2. **Drafted Outreach Communications**:
   - **Srouji Avocats**: Drafted a concise contact form submission focusing on partnering to offer comprehensive AI compliance audits, highlighting LexOculus's automated technical deep scans.
   - **Drewes Privacy Law**: Created a cold email targeting their existing AI inventory and risk assessment services. Emphasized resolving the bottleneck of manual technical architecture audits.
   - **Brinkhof (Niels Arnold)**: Tailored an outreach email specifically for IT and outsourcing contracts, addressing the hidden AI risks in third-party software and vendor dependency. Also provided an alternative version optimized for his secretary/assistant.
   - **Faros.eu (Karolien Francken)**: Designed a targeted email focusing on her role in compliance and M&A due diligence, positioning LexOculus as the technical evidence layer for their compliance audits.

3. **Outreach Best Practices Discussed**:
   - Advised against sending the exact same email to multiple partners at the same boutique firm (multi-threading) to avoid looking like spam and causing internal confusion. Recommended a "Single Point of Contact" or "Top-Down Referral" approach instead.

## Next Steps
- Send out the drafted communications to the respective law firms.
- Monitor responses and adjust the outreach messaging based on initial feedback.
- Consider creating a dedicated landing page (`/partners` or `/law-firms`) tailored specifically for B2B legal partners.
